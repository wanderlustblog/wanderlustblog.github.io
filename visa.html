Visa & Travel Requirements, Simplified

Every country has its own rules. Here's the practical, no-fluff breakdown of visa types, documents, and timelines — based on what we've actually navigated on our own trips.

Before you book

Know Before You Go

Tourist Visa

Short-stay entry for leisure travel, usually 30–90 days. Required in advance for many countries; some offer visa-on-arrival or e-visas.

1–3 weeks

E-Visa

Applied for entirely online before you fly. Faster and simpler than an embassy visit, available for a growing number of countries.

3–7 days

Visa-on-Arrival

Issued at the airport or border on entry. No advance application needed, but usually requires cash or card payment on the spot.

Same day

Digital Nomad Visa

For remote workers staying longer term. Typically needs proof of income, remote employment, and health insurance.

4–8 weeks

Student Visa

Required for enrolled study abroad. Needs an acceptance letter, proof of funds, and often a health check or interview.

4–12 weeks

Transit Visa

Needed for layovers in some countries even if you're not leaving the airport. Always check based on your layover country and duration.

1–5 days

Always double-check official sources

Visa rules change often and depend heavily on your passport's nationality. Confirm current requirements directly with the destination country's embassy or official immigration website before booking flights.
