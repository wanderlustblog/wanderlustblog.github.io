Harbin Ice and Snow Festival 2026-27: Dates, Venues, Tickets and What to Wear

October 26, 202612 min readby WanderLust Blog

Harbin turns a frozen northern city into the world's largest ice playground, and the trip works well when you plan for the cold first and the sightseeing second.

The Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival is held in the capital of China's northeastern Heilongjiang province. Massive buildings and towers are carved from blocks of ice cut from the frozen Songhua River, lit in bright colours at night and surrounded by ice slides, shows and food stalls. The 28th edition was announced in October 2026 with a record area of 1.25 million square metres. This guide covers dates, the three main venues, how to buy tickets, how to dress and how to plan a four-day trip.

Harbin Ice Festival 2026-27 Dates: What We Know

No official opening date or ticket price list had been published when this guide was written. Travel guides expect Harbin Ice and Snow World to open around December 20, 2026 and the festival to run until late February 2027, with January the peak month. The previous edition officially opened on January 5, 2026, and Ice and Snow World stayed open until February 21, 2026. Because exact dates can shift, book flights and hotels with free cancellation until the organisers publish the official schedule. Check the official festival announcements, the Harbin tourism bureau and trusted travel agents for confirmed dates.

The Three Main Venues

Harbin Ice and Snow World

This is the headline attraction and what most people mean by the Harbin Ice Festival. It is a large theme park of ice buildings, towers, slides and performances, open from about 11 am to 10 pm every day. It is best seen at dusk and after dark, when the ice glows. It is also the most expensive ticket, and queues for slides can be long on weekends and holidays.

Sun Island International Snow Sculpture Art Expo

Sun Island is known for giant snow sculptures created by artists from around the world, set in a large park across the Songhua River. It is best seen in daylight, when the details are clear, and is a good counterpart to the night-time ice displays.

Zhaolin Park Ice Lantern Garden

Zhaolin Park has a long tradition of ice lanterns, with smaller sculptures lit from within. It is easy to reach from the city centre and is usually cheaper than the larger venues. It suits an evening walk.

How to Buy Tickets Safely

  • Wait for the official price list, since prices vary by date and by ticket type, and last season's figures are only a guide.
  • Buy from the official ticket channels, major travel platforms or reputable agents, and keep a digital confirmation.
  • Check what is included. Some tickets exclude ice slides, rides or shows, and VIP options may offer shorter queues.
  • Avoid unofficial street sellers, since fake tickets and inflated prices are common in tourist areas.
  • Weekday visits are generally less crowded than weekends and public holidays.

A 4-Day Harbin Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive and walk Central Street

Check in, rest and adjust to the cold. In the afternoon, walk Zhongyang (Central) Street, a pedestrian street with European-style buildings, and visit St. Sophia Cathedral. Eat early and keep the evening light.

Day 2: Sun Island and Zhaolin Park

Visit Sun Island in the morning or early afternoon, return to the hotel for a warm break, and then see the Zhaolin Park ice lanterns in the evening.

Day 3: Harbin Ice and Snow World

Arrive in the late afternoon to see the sunset transition, and stay through the evening lighting. Allow several hours, and take breaks in heated areas.

Day 4: Extra options and departure

Choose a Siberian tiger park visit, a ski day at a nearby resort such as Yabuli, or a quiet morning at a museum or café. Leave a buffer for the airport or train station, as snow can delay transport.

Getting to Harbin and Getting Around

Harbin has an international airport and is connected by high-speed rail to other major Chinese cities. Check current flight and rail options from your location, and book early for January and the Lunar New Year period, when demand rises; see our Lunar New Year 2027 guide. In the city, metro, buses and taxis are available, but cold weather can slow taxis and drivers may be reluctant to take short trips. Hotel staff can call a taxi or arrange a car with a driver for festival venues. Download offline maps and a translation app, since English is limited.

Visas, Payments and Connectivity

Entry rules for China change often and vary by nationality, so check the official embassy or consulate site and any visa-free or transit schemes before you book. For payments, mobile payment apps such as Alipay and WeChat Pay dominate, and many foreign visitors link an international card to one of them before arrival. Carry some cash and a card as backup. Check the rules for mobile data, VPN and messaging apps in China, and consider an eSIM or local SIM. Keep copies of your passport and hotel booking, since you may be asked for them at hotels and venues.

What to Wear in Extreme Cold

Night temperatures can reach about minus 30 degrees Celsius, and wind makes it feel colder. Dress in layers and protect every exposed area.

  • Base layer: thermal top and leggings in merino or synthetic fabric, not cotton.
  • Middle layer: fleece or wool, plus a second warm layer for very cold nights.
  • Outer layer: a long, windproof down or insulated coat that covers your hips and ideally your knees.
  • Feet: insulated, waterproof boots with a grippy sole, plus thick wool socks. Many tourists rent or buy boots locally.
  • Head, face and hands: a warm hat, balaclava or neck gaiter, ski gloves with liner gloves, and chemical hand and foot warmers.
  • Extras: lip balm, moisturiser and sunglasses for snow glare.

See our winter packing list for a general layering system, and add extra insulation for Harbin.

Cold-Weather Safety

  • Take breaks in heated buildings every 30 to 45 minutes to avoid frostbite.
  • Keep batteries warm in inner pockets, because phones and cameras die quickly in the cold.
  • Avoid touching metal with bare skin, and do not lick or press your tongue on cold surfaces.
  • Walk carefully on icy pavements, using short steps and a wider stance.
  • Watch children closely, as they cool faster than adults.

Food to Try

Harbin is known for hearty northeastern Chinese dishes, including dumplings, braised meats, pickled cabbage stews, grilled skewers, sausages and Russian-influenced bread and sweets. Frozen fruit coated in sugar syrup, often served on a stick, is a popular winter treat. Check dietary requirements with a translation card, and choose busy places with high turnover.

Budget Planning Without Guesswork

Because prices were not published when this guide was written, build your budget from categories: flights or rail, hotel per night, festival tickets, local transport, food, clothing rental or purchase, insurance and a 10 to 15 percent buffer. Hotels near the festival rise in price during peak weeks. Compare two or three areas, and check whether breakfast, heating and airport transfers are included.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking non-refundable flights before the opening date is confirmed.
  • Underestimating the cold and wearing fashion boots.
  • Visiting Ice and Snow World in the middle of the day, when the lights are not yet lit.
  • Relying on a foreign-only payment method without a backup.
  • Trying to see every venue in a single day.

Final Checklist

  • Visa and entry rules confirmed and booking terms flexible.
  • Hotel near the venues, with heating and hot water confirmed.
  • Extreme-cold clothing, hand warmers and spare batteries packed.
  • A payment app, offline maps and a translation tool set up before you fly.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the Harbin Ice Festival 2026-27 open?

No official date had been published at the time of writing. Guides expect Harbin Ice and Snow World to open around December 20, 2026 and run until late February 2027.

What are the main Harbin Ice Festival venues?

Harbin Ice and Snow World, the Sun Island Snow Expo and the Zhaolin Park ice lantern garden.

How cold is Harbin during the festival?

Night temperatures can reach about minus 30 degrees Celsius, so proper layering and insulated boots are essential.

What is the best time of day to visit Ice and Snow World?

Arrive in the late afternoon and stay through dusk and evening, when the lights make the ice glow.

Do I need a visa to visit Harbin?

It depends on your nationality, and rules change often, so check the official Chinese embassy or consulate site before booking.

How many days do I need?

Three to four days is a comfortable plan for the three main venues and the city.
