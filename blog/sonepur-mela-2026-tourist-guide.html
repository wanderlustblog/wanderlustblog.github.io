Sonepur Mela 2026 Tourist Guide: Dates, How to Reach from Patna, Stay and Safety

October 28, 202612 min readby WanderLust Blog

Sonepur Mela is one of India's oldest and largest fairs, held where the Gandak meets the Ganga, and it is still surprisingly easy to see without a crowd of other tourists.

Sonepur Mela, also called the Harihar Kshetra Mela, takes place at Sonepur in Bihar, at the meeting of the Gandak and Ganga rivers. It is often described as one of the largest cattle and livestock fairs in Asia, and it has been held for centuries. Today it combines a holy bath on Kartik Purnima, a very large fair with crafts, food and entertainment, and tourism arrangements run by the state government. For visitors, it is a rare chance to see a traditional fair in a less touristy part of India. This guide explains the dates, how to reach the fair from Patna, where to stay, what to see and how to stay safe.

Sonepur Mela 2026 Dates

Sonepur Mela traditionally begins around Kartik Purnima, which falls on Tuesday, November 24, 2026. A local report from a district review meeting in Saran says the fair is expected to open earlier, on November 22, with the main Kartik Purnima celebrations on November 23 and 24. Other listings suggest the fair runs for roughly a month, in some cases until about December 23. Official dates and the closing date are announced by the Bihar government and Bihar tourism, so treat these as expected rather than confirmed, and check the official announcements closer to the date. The holy bath at the confluence on Kartik Purnima is the spiritual centre of the fair, so that is the best day for atmosphere and also the busiest.

What Sonepur Mela Is Like

  • Holy dip at the confluence: on Kartik Purnima, devotees bathe at dawn where the two rivers meet, and visit the Harihar Nath temple, which is dedicated to the combined form of Vishnu and Shiva.
  • The fairground: a huge temporary market with stalls selling crafts, textiles, household goods, toys, food and sweets, plus rides and entertainment.
  • Livestock: the fair has a long history as a livestock market, historically with cattle, horses and elephants. Today the number and type of animals sold is reduced and regulated, and animal-trade rules have changed.
  • Cultural programmes: the state tourism department organises folk music, dance and art events, and has set up a craft village and tourist information facilities in recent years.
  • Tourism packages: in past seasons the tourism department has offered packages that include a vehicle, guide and an overnight stay in a temporary tourism village. Check if this is available for 2026.

How to Reach Sonepur from Patna

Patna is the best base, with an airport, a major railway station and good road links. Sonepur is roughly 25 km from Patna across the Ganga, and the road trip typically takes about an hour, but traffic is heavy around the fair. You can take a taxi, hire a driver for the day, or use a local bus or shared vehicle. Hajipur, on the opposite bank, is also well connected by rail. During the fair, special trains or extra services may operate, so check with Indian Railways. For booking tips, see our guide to using trains in India as a foreigner.

A 1-Day Plan from Patna

Morning: Leave early

Leave Patna by 6:30 or 7 am to avoid the heaviest traffic and to reach Sonepur while the day is still cool. If you plan to see the dawn bath on Kartik Purnima, you will need to start much earlier or stay overnight nearby.

Late morning: Harihar Nath temple and the confluence

Visit the temple, watch the river activity, and see the ghats. Dress modestly, keep your voice low and ask before photographing devotees.

Afternoon: The fairground

Walk through the fairground, try local snacks, browse crafts and watch performances. Keep your valuables secure, and agree on prices before buying.

Evening: Return before dark

Aim to leave before the evening rush. If you stay late, the ghat lit with lamps is a beautiful sight, but plan transport back to Patna in advance.

Where to Stay

  • Hotels in Patna: the most comfortable choice, with a range of budgets. You will need to commute on fair days.
  • Government tourism village or cottages at the fair: a more immersive option, with limited availability. Book early through official Bihar tourism channels, and check what is included.
  • Guesthouses and homestays near Sonepur or Hajipur: basic but close. Check reviews and safety.

Combining Sonepur With Other Kartik Purnima Events

The same full moon is the centre of several other celebrations, including the Pushkar Camel Fair and Dev Deepawali in Varanasi. You cannot be at all of them on the same night, so choose one for the full moon day and visit another earlier or later. Read our hub, Kartik Purnima 2026: where to go in India, to compare them.

Animal Welfare and Responsible Visiting

Livestock fairs raise real animal-welfare questions. Choose to watch respectfully, do not encourage mistreatment, and avoid rides or photo opportunities that look stressful for animals. Follow the rules set by the authorities, and support local artisans and food vendors instead. Do not buy items made from protected wildlife.

Safety and Practical Tips

  • Expect very large crowds on and around Kartik Purnima. Stay with your group and agree a meeting point.
  • Keep valuables minimal, carry a copy of your ID, and use inner pockets.
  • Drink bottled water, and eat freshly cooked food from busy stalls.
  • Wear sturdy shoes, since the fair ground can be dusty and uneven.
  • See our scams guide for common traps at fairs and pilgrimage sites.
  • Bathing in the river is for devotees; if you choose to bathe, stay in shallow, supervised areas.

Weather and What to Wear

Late November in Bihar is dry and pleasantly cool, with chilly mornings and evenings and occasional fog. Wear light layers, a warm shawl or jacket, comfortable closed shoes, and modest clothing. Bring sunscreen, a power bank, tissues, hand sanitiser and basic medicines. See what to wear in India for more tips.

Food to Try in Bihar

Bihari food is hearty and distinctive. Try litti chokha (roasted wheat balls with spiced mashed vegetables), sattu drinks and parathas, local sweets such as khaja and thekua, and fresh seasonal snacks at the fair. Vegetarian options are widely available. If you have allergies, ask about ingredients and oil.

Budget Planning Without Guesswork

Entry to the fairground is generally free, so costs come from travel, stay and food. Build a list of transport to Patna, local taxis, accommodation, meals, souvenirs, any tourism package and a buffer of 10 to 15 percent. Compare options for your dates, and check cancellation terms. Prices rise around Kartik Purnima.

More Questions Visitors Ask

Is Sonepur Mela suitable for foreign tourists?

Yes, with common-sense preparation. It is less touristy than Pushkar, so expect fewer English signs and more crowds. Go with a driver or guide for the first visit.

Is it safe for solo women?

Large fairs can be crowded, so stay in busy, well-lit areas, avoid going out alone after dark and use trusted transport. Dress modestly and keep valuables secure.

How many days do I need?

One day is enough from Patna, but two days gives you time to see Patna's sights and the fair without rushing.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Assuming the fair dates are fixed and booking non-refundable travel too early.
  • Travelling on Kartik Purnima without a plan for traffic and crowds.
  • Photographing devotees or animals without permission.
  • Underestimating travel time in heavy traffic.

Final Checklist

  • Official dates checked on Bihar tourism announcements.
  • Patna accommodation and transport booked, with flexible terms.
  • Modest clothing, warm layers, power bank and basic medicines packed.
  • A plan for crowds, meeting points and the return journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Sonepur Mela 2026?

It is expected to open around November 22, with the main celebrations on Kartik Purnima, November 24, and it traditionally runs for about a month. Check official Bihar announcements.

Where is Sonepur Mela held?

At Harihar Kshetra in Sonepur, Saran district, Bihar, where the Gandak meets the Ganga.

How far is Sonepur from Patna?

Roughly 25 km, about an hour by road in normal traffic, but slower during the fair.

Is Sonepur Mela safe for tourists?

Generally yes, with crowd precautions. Keep valuables secure, stay in groups and use reliable transport.

Where can I stay for Sonepur Mela?

Patna hotels are the most comfortable, while the state tourism village and nearby guesthouses offer closer options.

Can I visit in one day?

Yes, from Patna. Start early and return before the evening rush.
