Kartik Purnima 2026: Where to Go in India on November 24 (Pushkar, Varanasi, Sonepur and More)

October 28, 202612 min readby WanderLust Blog

One full moon, five very different festivals: on November 24, 2026, Kartik Purnima turns Pushkar, Varanasi, Sonepur and several other places into pilgrimage and festival centres at the same time.

Kartik Purnima is the full moon of the Hindu month of Kartik, and across India it is a day of holy baths, lamp-lighting and large fairs. In 2026 it falls on Tuesday, November 24. It is one of the best days of the year to see Indian festival culture, but because many celebrations happen at the same time, you need to choose one place. This guide compares the main options so you can pick the one that suits your interests and your travel style, and avoid the common mistake of trying to do too much.

What Is Kartik Purnima?

Kartik Purnima is considered especially auspicious for bathing in sacred rivers and lakes, lighting lamps and visiting temples. In many places it marks the end of a month of devotion, and in some regions it is also associated with the festival of lamps known as Dev Deepawali, and with Guru Nanak's birth anniversary in Sikh tradition. The exact timing of rituals depends on the lunar calendar, so check local announcements for the right hours.

The Main Places to Be on Kartik Purnima 2026

1. Varanasi: Dev Deepawali

The ghats of Varanasi are lit with a very large number of earthen lamps, boats fill the river and the evening aarti is a spectacle. It is the best choice if you want lights and photographs, and the most crowded. Read our Dev Deepawali Varanasi 2026 guide.

2. Pushkar: the end of the Camel Fair

The Pushkar fair peaks around Kartik Purnima, when pilgrims bathe in the sacred lake. It is the best choice for culture, photography and a mix of pilgrimage and market atmosphere. See our Pushkar Camel Fair 2026 guide.

3. Sonepur: Asia's famous cattle fair

At the confluence of the Gandak and Ganga in Bihar, the Sonepur Mela begins around Kartik Purnima. It is the best choice if you want an off-the-beaten-track fair with fewer tourists. Read our Sonepur Mela 2026 guide.

4. Kolayat, Rajasthan

Kolayat holds a fair around a sacred lake linked to the sage Kapil, with pilgrims bathing and lighting lamps. It is quieter than Pushkar and is a good choice for a calm, local experience. Check the Rajasthan tourism announcements for dates and arrangements.

5. Garhmukteshwar, Uttar Pradesh

This town on the Ganga hosts a large bathing fair on Kartik Purnima, mostly attended by local and regional pilgrims. It is less suited to first-time foreign visitors, but interesting for travellers who want a very local experience.

Other celebrations

In south India, the same day is linked to Karthigai Deepam, with a famous lamp-lighting at Tiruvannamalai; see our Karthigai Deepam 2026 guide. In Thailand, the nearby full moon is the time of Loy Krathong; read our Loy Krathong guide.

A Sample 7-Day Route Around Kartik Purnima

Option A: Rajasthan route

Fly into Jaipur, spend two nights there, travel to Pushkar for three nights around the full moon, and finish in Udaipur or Delhi. This is the easiest route for first-time visitors.

Option B: Uttar Pradesh and Bihar route

Fly into Delhi, travel to Varanasi, spend the full moon night there, and optionally continue to Patna and Sonepur on the following days. Plan buffer time, since long distances and fog can delay trains.

Planning Tips That Apply Everywhere

  • Book early. Hotels and trains sell out around Kartik Purnima. See our guide to using trains in India.
  • Arrive the day before. Roads close and traffic builds on the main day.
  • Dress modestly and respectfully. See what to wear in India.
  • Protect yourself in crowds. Keep valuables minimal and agree on a meeting point.
  • Check official dates. Fairs and programmes are announced by state governments.

Weather and Packing

Late November is dry and pleasant across most of north and central India, with cool mornings and evenings. Bring light layers, a shawl or jacket, comfortable shoes, sunscreen and a power bank. In Tamil Nadu, expect warmer weather and possible rain.

More Questions Visitors Ask

Can I visit two places on Kartik Purnima?

Not practically on the same day, because of distance, traffic and crowds. Visit one on the full moon and another before or after.

Which is best for first-timers?

Pushkar is the easiest, with good tourist facilities. Varanasi is more intense but unforgettable.

Which is least crowded?

Kolayat and Sonepur are usually less visited by tourists, though still crowded with local pilgrims.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Trying to visit multiple festivals on the same day.
  • Booking late, which leaves poor or expensive options.
  • Relying on unofficial sources for dates and timings.
  • Ignoring local rules about photography and bathing.

Final Checklist

  • One destination chosen for November 24, and a backup plan.
  • Accommodation and transport booked with flexible terms.
  • Modest clothing, warm layers and a power bank packed.
  • Official dates and timings checked shortly before you go.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Kartik Purnima 2026?

Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

Where is the best place to celebrate Kartik Purnima in India?

Varanasi for lights, Pushkar for culture and Sonepur for an off-beat fair.

Is Kartik Purnima the same as Dev Deepawali?

Dev Deepawali, celebrated in Varanasi, falls on Kartik Purnima.

Can I attend more than one festival?

Choose one for the full moon day and visit another earlier or later.

Is it crowded?

Yes. Major places are very crowded, so book early and avoid driving on the main day.

How early should I book?

As early as possible, ideally several weeks ahead.
