Is India Safe for Australian Tourists? What Smartraveller Actually Says

September 4, 20267 min readby WanderLust Blog

Australia is a genuinely significant source of visitors to India, and most of what trips up first-timers from Australia specifically comes down to two things: understanding what Smartraveller's rating actually means, and a couple of entry-form requirements that catch people out.

What Smartraveller Actually Says

The Australian Government's Smartraveller currently rates India at Level 2: Exercise a high degree of caution, citing the threat of terrorism and crime, and the risk of civil unrest. This is a genuinely important distinction to understand correctly:

  • The national Level 2 rating applies broadly, but specific border and conflict-affected regions — including parts of Jammu and Kashmir and areas near the India–Pakistan border, along with some northeastern states — carry higher, more specific Level 3 ("Reconsider your need to travel") or Level 4 ("Do not travel") warnings.
  • Major tourist hubs commonly visited on a first trip — Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Mumbai, and similar destinations — generally sit at the national Level 2 rating rather than the elevated regional warnings.
  • Travel to Level 4 areas typically voids most travel insurance policies, which makes checking Smartraveller's interactive map against your specific itinerary a genuinely practical step, not just a formality.
  • Smartraveller recommends registering your trip through their website to receive updates relevant to your travel dates and destinations.

Check Smartraveller's India page directly before finalizing your itinerary, since it's updated periodically and regional guidance can shift, sometimes with limited notice around specific events.

Extra Entry Requirements Australians Need to Know

Beyond the standard e-Tourist Visa covered in our broader guide, there are two additional steps currently required based on official guidance:

  • Australian citizens and OCI cardholders are currently required to complete an online e-Arrival Card up to three days before travel to India, and depending on your travel route, a paper arrival card may still be requested as well.
  • A separate online health self-declaration form is currently required up to 24 hours before arrival, related to health screening measures — not completing it in advance can mean delays and additional screening at Indian immigration.

These requirements are updated periodically, so confirm the current process on India's official visa portal and Smartraveller's India page shortly before you travel, rather than relying solely on this guide.

Getting There: Flights from Australia

Several airlines connect major Australian cities — including Sydney, Melbourne, Perth, and Brisbane — to Delhi and Mumbai, either directly or via a single connection through hubs like Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, or the Gulf. Flight times vary considerably depending on your departure city and routing, so it's worth comparing options carefully rather than assuming a fixed duration. Given the distance, building a lighter first day or two into your itinerary to adjust before diving into intensive sightseeing is a sensible approach — our India itinerary guide for first-time visitors covers how to structure that time.

This window also fits reasonably well around the Australian school holiday calendar for travelers planning around family trips, though it's worth checking specific dates against your own state's calendar.

Power Adapters: One Detail Worth Getting Right

Australia uses Type I plugs, while India uses Type C, D, and M — the voltage (230V in both countries) is compatible, so it's the plug shape rather than voltage conversion that Australian travelers need to plan for. A universal travel adapter covers this without any extra thought.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is India safe for Australian tourists?

Smartraveller currently rates India at Level 2: Exercise a high degree of caution, due to the threat of terrorism, crime, and civil unrest, with more specific higher-risk warnings for particular border regions well outside standard tourist circuits like Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur.

What extra entry forms do Australians need for India?

Currently, an online e-Arrival Card (up to three days before travel) and a health self-declaration form (up to 24 hours before arrival) — confirm current requirements on the official visa portal and Smartraveller close to your travel date.

Should I register my trip with Smartraveller before traveling to India?

It's recommended — registering helps you receive relevant updates for your specific destinations and travel dates.

Does travel insurance cover trips to India for Australians?

Generally yes for standard tourist itineraries at the national Level 2 rating, but check your specific policy carefully if your itinerary includes any Level 3 or Level 4 regions, since these can void coverage.

Will my Australian power adapter work in India?

No — Australia uses Type I plugs and India uses Type C, D, and M, so a universal travel adapter is needed, though voltage (230V) is compatible between the two.
