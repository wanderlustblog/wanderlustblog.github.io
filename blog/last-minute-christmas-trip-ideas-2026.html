Last-Minute Christmas Trip Ideas 2026: How to Still Plan a Good Trip

October 22, 20267 min readby WanderLust Blog

Booking late narrows your options, but it doesn't have to ruin the trip. The trick is to choose flexibility over a specific destination.

Quick TakeDetails
Best strategyBe flexible on dates and destination
Look forShort breaks, nearby cities, road or rail trips
AvoidPeak-demand destinations at peak dates
AlwaysCheck refund and change rules

Late planners often make the mistake of trying to book a famous destination for the exact peak days. A better approach is to widen the search and let availability guide you.

Be Flexible on Dates

Christmas week is the most expensive and sold-out period almost everywhere. Shifting your trip by a few days either side often opens up better options and fewer crowds. Flights on and around Christmas Day itself are often cheaper than the days before, though this varies by route.

Think Nearby

A short break by train or car removes the biggest cost and availability problem, flights. Compare city breaks, Christmas market towns and cosy countryside stays within a few hours of home.

Ideas by Trip Type

  • Festive city break: a Christmas market city such as Strasbourg, Vienna or Dresden; see our Europe markets guide.
  • Warm escape: compare options in our warm December destinations list.
  • Snow trip: consider mountain towns that may still have rooms.
  • Quiet stay: a cabin or countryside hotel can feel more Christmassy than a crowded city.

Booking Tips

  • Check cancellation and change terms before you pay.
  • Compare total cost, including transfers, baggage and food.
  • Book accommodation first in popular places.
  • Check entry and visa rules early if you're going abroad.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it too late to book a Christmas trip in 2026?

No, but options narrow as December nears, so be flexible on dates and destination.

Are last-minute Christmas trips expensive?

Often, especially for peak dates, but flexibility and nearby destinations can reduce costs.

What is the best last-minute Christmas trip?

A short city or Christmas-market break reachable by train or car is usually easiest.

Should I travel on Christmas Day?

Flights around Christmas Day are often cheaper than the surrounding days, but check each route.

What should I check before booking late?

Cancellation terms, total cost, visa or entry rules, and accommodation availability.

Can I still find warm weather?

Yes, but beachfront rooms in popular areas may be gone, so compare several destinations.
