Sapporo Snow Festival 2027: Dates, Sites, Otaru Overlap and How to Plan

October 26, 202612 min readby WanderLust Blog

Sapporo's snow festival turns a modern city park into a gallery of huge snow sculptures, and a little planning makes the difference between a magical trip and a cold, crowded one.

The Sapporo Snow Festival is held every February in Hokkaido's capital and draws visitors from across Japan and the world. Giant snow and ice sculptures, some several storeys high, fill the city centre, with lights and projection shows after dark. In 2027 the festival runs from February 4 to February 11, spread across three sites. This guide explains the sites, how to pair the festival with Otaru, what to wear, where to stay and how to make a simple three-day plan.

Sapporo Snow Festival 2027: Dates at a Glance

The festival is scheduled for eight days, from Thursday, February 4 to Thursday, February 11, 2027. Sculpture displays are typically open from morning to late evening, with lighting after dark, and some sculptures can melt or be removed early if the weather turns warm. Check the official festival website for the 2027 programme, opening hours, the schedule of the International Snow Sculpture Contest and any ticketed events. Weekends and the national holiday on February 11 are the busiest times. If your dates are flexible, weekdays in the middle of the festival are calmer.

The Three Festival Sites

Odori Site: the main attraction

Odori Park runs through the centre of the city, and the main sculptures line a long stretch of it. This is where you will see the largest displays, international sculpture contest entries and the evening light and projection shows. It is free to enter, and easy to reach on foot from the central subway stations.

Susukino Site: ice sculptures after dark

Susukino is Sapporo's entertainment district, and the festival here focuses on illuminated ice sculptures along the main street. It is best after dark, combined with dinner and drinks.

Tsudome Site: family fun

The Tsudome site is a short ride from the city centre and is designed for families, with snow slides, snow rafting and play areas. It is the best place for children and for people who want to play in the snow rather than only look at sculptures.

Pairing Sapporo With Otaru

The Otaru Snow Light Path Festival, in the nearby port city of Otaru, overlaps with the Sapporo festival from about February 6 to 11, 2027. Candlelit snow lanterns line the canal and old streets, creating a very different, quieter atmosphere. Otaru is roughly half an hour to forty minutes from Sapporo by train, so it works as an evening trip. Plan to go on a weekday evening if you can, and expect crowds on the canal at peak times. Check the official Otaru programme for dates and hours.

A Simple 3-Day Plan

Day 1: Arrive and see Odori by night

Check in, adjust to the cold and walk Odori Park after dark to see the sculptures lit up. Eat dinner nearby and keep the evening relaxed.

Day 2: Tsudome in the morning, Susukino at night

Visit Tsudome in the morning if you are with children or enjoy snow play, and return to the city for lunch. See Susukino's ice sculptures in the evening, and have dinner in the district.

Day 3: Otaru or a Hokkaido day trip

Take a train to Otaru, walk the canal and old warehouses by day, and see the snow lanterns in the evening. Return to Sapporo by a late train, checking timetables in advance.

Where to Stay and When to Book

Sapporo is crowded during the festival, and hotel prices rise. Book as early as possible, ideally several months ahead, and choose a hotel near a subway station on the Namboku or Tozai lines for easy access to the sites. Staying in or near the Odori or Susukino areas puts you within walking distance, but rooms there are the most expensive. Consider hotels near Sapporo Station as an alternative. Check cancellation terms, and compare apartments and guesthouses if you are travelling as a group.

Getting to Sapporo and Around

New Chitose Airport is the gateway to Hokkaido, and a train connects it to central Sapporo in under an hour. Winter weather can delay flights and trains, so leave a buffer around your arrival and departure. In Sapporo, the subway, trams and buses are efficient, and many festival sites are walkable. Heavy snow can slow buses and taxis, so plan more time than usual. Download offline maps and a transport app; see our offline maps guide. Check the visa requirements for your nationality before booking.

What to Wear in Sapporo in February

February in Sapporo is cold, with snowfall, icy streets and strong windchill, though it is usually less extreme than Harbin. Dress in layers.

  • Base layer: thermal top and leggings, preferably merino or synthetic fabric.
  • Middle layer: fleece or wool sweater.
  • Outer layer: a warm, waterproof and windproof coat with a hood.
  • Footwear: waterproof snow boots with deep tread. Add slip-on ice grips (small spikes) if you plan to walk much on packed snow or ice.
  • Accessories: hat, scarf or neck gaiter, insulated gloves, warm socks and hand warmers.

Food to Try in Sapporo

Hokkaido is known for fresh seafood, dairy, soup curry, miso ramen and grilled lamb served as Genghis Khan. Look for hot street snacks at the festival, such as grilled corn, potatoes with butter, and warm sweet drinks. Reserve restaurants for dinner in Susukino during festival week, since popular places fill up. If you have dietary restrictions, learn the Japanese phrases for common allergens, or carry a translation card.

Winter Walking and Safety

  • Walk with short steps and a slightly wider stance, and keep your hands out of your pockets for balance.
  • Be careful on tram tracks, near crossings and on slopes where ice builds up.
  • Watch for falling snow and icicles from roofs and eaves.
  • Keep phones and cameras warm, since cold drains batteries quickly.
  • Take breaks in heated cafés and shops, and drink warm fluids.

Budget Planning Without Guesswork

Prices vary by date and fluctuate with demand. Build your own budget by listing flights or rail, hotels for each night, local transport, food, optional activities, winter clothing or rental, insurance and a 10 to 15 percent buffer. Compare weekday and weekend hotel rates, and check whether breakfast is included. Many festival attractions are free, so most spending goes on travel and accommodation.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking late and paying high prices or settling for a poor location.
  • Wearing boots without grip, which makes walking tiring and unsafe.
  • Visiting only during the day and missing the evening lights.
  • Scheduling a tight same-day flight home after the closing day.
  • Underestimating transport delays in snowy weather.

Combine It With Other Winter Trips

If you are planning a winter festival trip across Asia, compare Sapporo with Harbin's Ice and Snow Festival, which is colder and larger in scale, and with Lunar New Year travel in early February. For a snowy Arctic option, see our Rovaniemi guide.

Final Checklist

  • Official 2027 programme and opening hours checked.
  • Hotels and transport booked early, with cancellation terms.
  • Waterproof boots with ice grips, thermals and a windproof coat packed.
  • A plan for Odori at night, Tsudome by day and an Otaru evening.

More Questions Visitors Ask About Sapporo

Is the festival suitable for young children?

Yes, particularly the Tsudome site, which has slides and play areas. Dress children in extra layers, take warm-up breaks and avoid late evenings in the coldest hours.

What happens if the weather is warm or it rains?

Sculptures can soften or be partly closed for safety. Check the official website and local weather forecast, and keep indoor alternatives such as museums, food halls and hot springs in mind.

Can I visit in a single day?

You can see Odori and Susukino in one day, but a second day gives time for Tsudome or Otaru. Two nights is a comfortable minimum.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Sapporo Snow Festival 2027?

It runs from February 4 to February 11, 2027.

Where are the Sapporo Snow Festival sites?

Odori Park, Susukino and Tsudome.

Is the Sapporo Snow Festival free?

The main Odori and Susukino sites are generally free to enter, while some activities and events may charge. Check the official programme.

Can I visit Otaru during the festival?

Yes. The Otaru Snow Light Path overlaps from about February 6 to 11, 2027, and Otaru is a short train ride from Sapporo.

What should I wear in Sapporo in February?

Thermal layers, a waterproof and windproof coat, snow boots with deep tread, ice grips, a hat, gloves and a scarf.

How many days do I need?

Two to three days covers the main sites and an evening trip to Otaru.
