Who Can Use the Morocco E-Visa?

According to the guides we read, Indian passport holders can apply for a Morocco e-visa online for tourism, with a maximum stay of 30 days per visit. A country listing for Indian citizens also shows Morocco as an e-visa destination with a 30-day stay. One visa-service page states that Indians do not need a US, UK or Schengen visa to qualify and that visa on arrival is not available to Indians.

That one detail matters, because older web pages describe conditions that no longer apply. We could not confirm the eligibility rules from an official Moroccan page, so check the current wording on the official portal before you book flights. If you are unsure, ask the Moroccan embassy or consulate that serves you.

Step-by-Step: How to Apply

Follow these steps in order. They match the process described by the guide we read.

  1. Check your passport. It should be valid for at least 90 days from the date you apply, and ideally much longer. Check your other visa-free options if your passport is close to expiry.
  2. Go to the official portal only. Type www.acces-maroc.ma yourself instead of clicking an advert. Third-party sites may charge extra.
  3. Create an account and verify your email. Use an email address you check daily, because the e-visa arrives there.
  4. Fill in the form. Enter personal, passport and travel details exactly as they appear in your passport.
  5. Upload the documents. You need the passport bio-data page and a recent digital passport photo.
  6. Pay and choose your speed. Choose standard or express. Pay by card and keep the receipt.
  7. Download the e-visa PDF. It arrives by email. Save a copy to your phone and cloud storage.
  8. Print two copies. The guide says digital copies are not accepted at border control, so carry paper.

Documents to Prepare

For the application itself, the list is short. For the trip, prepare a little more, because airlines and immigration officers may ask questions even if the portal does not.

For the portal

Passport bio-data page scan, a recent digital passport-style photo, a valid email address and a credit or debit card.

For the airline

Printed e-visa, your passport and a confirmed onward or return ticket. Airlines often check entry papers before boarding.

For immigration

Hotel or riad booking details, a return ticket and enough funds. Our sources do not say what border officers ask for, so carry these as a precaution.

For your trip

Travel insurance. Read our insurance guide for Indians for what to look for.

The guide says flight tickets, hotel bookings and bank statements are not required when you apply, so you can usually get your visa before booking non-refundable travel. That is useful, but confirm it on the portal.

When to Apply

The guide suggests applying at least 10 days before departure. Standard processing is 72 hours in Moroccan working days, which means weekends and holidays can stretch it. A safer plan is to apply two to three weeks before you fly, which leaves room for payment problems or corrections.

The e-visa is valid for 180 days from issue, so you do not need to apply months ahead. Choose a date after you have your flights in mind, but not so late that a delay would cost you a booking.

Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

  • Using an unofficial website. The guide warns that third-party sites charge higher fees. Always type the official address yourself.
  • Applying too late. The guide suggests at least 10 days before departure.
  • Giving up after a payment failure. The guide suggests retrying after 24 to 48 hours or switching browser or device, and not submitting multiple applications.
  • Not printing two copies. Digital copies are not accepted at border control, according to the guide.
  • Passport validity under 90 days. This is a common reason for problems.
  • Typing details wrongly. A spelling mismatch with your passport can cause trouble at check-in.
  • Assuming the fee is refundable. The guide says it is not, even if the application is refused.

What to Expect When You Land

Our sources describe the application, not the airport, so this section is general advice rather than reported fact. Keep your documents together in one folder so you can hand them over without digging through your bag.

  • Have paper ready. Two printed copies of the e-visa, your passport and your return ticket should be in your hand luggage, not your checked bag.
  • Know your address. Immigration forms commonly ask where you are staying, so keep your riad or hotel name, address and phone number handy.
  • Answer simply. Say you are visiting as a tourist, state how long you are staying and keep it short.
  • Download maps first. Save an offline map before you leave the airport. Our offline map guide explains which apps the travel guides recommend.
  • Agree taxi fares. Lonely Planet advises insisting on the meter or agreeing a price before you set off, and it gives fixed airport petit taxi fares of Dh80, or Dh120 at night, in an article dated November 2025.

How the Visa Fee Fits Into Your Budget

The visa is a small part of a Morocco trip. If the 770 MAD standard fee reported by one guide is right, that is roughly the price of an ordinary dinner and a few taxi rides in a city like Marrakech once you add them up, though prices vary. Day-to-day costs in the medina are modest: Lonely Planet's November 2025 figures put a coffee or mint tea at Dh6 to 12 at simple cafes, and a tagine at a street cafe at about Dh50. Our rooftop tea guide breaks down the cost of a view.

The sensible way to budget is to treat the visa fee, travel insurance and flights as fixed costs, then plan daily spending around your style. Do not pay a third-party site extra for something you can do yourself in half an hour.

Final Thoughts

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Indians get a Morocco e-visa online?

A country listing for Indian citizens shows Morocco as an e-visa destination with a 30-day stay, and a visa-service guide describes an online application on www.acces-maroc.ma. Confirm current rules on the official portal.

How much is the Morocco e-visa for Indians?

One visa-service guide updated in November 2026 reports 770 MAD for standard and 1,100 MAD for express, plus a 3% payment gateway charge. Fees can change, so check the official portal.

How long does the Morocco e-visa take?

The guide reports 72 hours for standard and 24 hours for express, counted in Moroccan working days. Apply at least 10 days before departure to allow for delays.

How long can I stay in Morocco on an e-visa?

Do I need to print the Morocco e-visa?

The guide says digital copies are not accepted at border control, so print two copies and also keep a digital copy.

Do I need hotel bookings and flight tickets to apply?

The guide says flight tickets, hotel bookings and bank statements are not required at application. Still carry them for the airline and immigration.
