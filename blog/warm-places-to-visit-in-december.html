Warm Places to Visit in December: Sunny Christmas Escapes and Where to Avoid

October 22, 20268 min readby WanderLust Blog

December is when the northern hemisphere trades coats for sunscreen, but only if you pick a destination in its dry season rather than its wet one.

BookingPeak pricing from Christmas to New Year

A beach Christmas is a different kind of holiday: less ritual, more rest. The key is matching dates to seasons, because the same island can be a postcard in February and a washout in December.

Destinations That Are Typically Dry and Sunny in December

  • Maldives: December falls in the drier northeast monsoon season, with sunnier skies and calmer seas.
  • Phuket and the Andaman coast (Thailand): the dry season starts around November and runs through the early months of the year.
  • Goa (India): warm, dry and low-humidity, which is why it is one of the most popular Christmas beach destinations; see our Christmas in Goa guide.
  • Sri Lanka's south and west coasts: December falls in the main season for beaches there.
  • Cape Town: December is early summer, with long days and good beach weather.

Places to Think Twice About in December

Some well-known tropical islands are in or near their wetter season in December. Bali is a common example; our Bali guide explains how the island changes by season. Fiji's wetter season, roughly November to April, overlaps with cyclone season, so check the forecast and have flexible bookings; our Fiji first-timer guide covers that in more detail.

How to Choose

  • Want quiet and luxury: the Maldives, but expect high prices over Christmas.
  • Want variety and value: Thailand's Andaman coast or Sri Lanka.
  • Want culture plus beaches: Goa, especially for its Christmas traditions.
  • Want city plus coast: Cape Town.

Booking Tips for Peak Weeks

Prices climb steeply between roughly December 20 and January 2, and the best beachfront rooms sell out first. If dates are flexible, travelling a few days before or after the peak usually saves money and avoids the thickest crowds.

How to Match Destination, Weather and Trip Style

The best warm destination for December depends on what you want to do. Use this quick guide to match your priorities to a place.

  • Beaches and relaxation: the Maldives, Phuket and the Andaman coast, Goa, or the south and west coasts of Sri Lanka.
  • Culture plus beaches: Goa, Kerala, southern Sri Lanka and Thailand combine heritage sites with coastlines.
  • City and nature: Cape Town offers mountains, beaches, wine regions and city life in summer weather.
  • Luxury and privacy: the Maldives, though prices are high in peak weeks.

Destination Snapshots

Maldives

December falls in the drier season, with sunny days and calmer seas. Resorts are usually on separate islands, so transfers by boat or seaplane are part of the cost. Book early for Christmas week and check what is included.

Thailand's Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi, Khao Lak)

The dry season starts around November, and December is popular. Beaches vary from lively to quiet. Book early for peak weeks, and check sea conditions for swimming and boat trips.

Goa

Warm, dry and affordable compared with many beach destinations, though Christmas to New Year is the busiest period. See our Goa Christmas guide.

Kerala

Pleasant weather, backwaters, beaches and hill stations. Read our Kerala Christmas guide for a route.

Sri Lanka

The south and west coasts are generally in their main beach season in December, with the central hills and wildlife parks offering additional options. Roads are slow, so keep your plan simple.

Cape Town and the Western Cape

Early summer brings long days and warm weather, though it can be windy. It is popular with visitors, so book accommodation early.

Where to Be Careful in December

Some tropical places are in a wetter season in December. Parts of Bali and much of Fiji, for example, can have rain, humidity and storm risk; see our Bali guide and Fiji guide for seasonal notes. If you still want those destinations, choose flexible bookings, plan indoor alternatives and check the forecast and local advisories.

Health, Safety and Insurance

  • Check vaccination and health advice for your destination, and carry basic medicines.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses, and stay hydrated.
  • Follow lifeguard flags and local advice about currents and swimming.
  • Choose travel insurance that covers cancellation, medical care and water activities.
  • Keep digital and paper copies of important documents.

Cost-Planning Tips

Peak weeks cost much more than the days either side. If you can, shift your trip to start after the busiest period or end before it. Compare total costs, including baggage, transfers, resort fees, taxes and meal plans. Check whether tours and activities need advance booking. Use refundable options when plans might change, and keep a buffer for unexpected costs.

A Simple 7-Day Beach Trip Template

  • Day 1: arrive, settle in and take a short evening walk.
  • Days 2 to 3: beach time and a gentle activity such as snorkelling or a boat trip.
  • Day 4: a cultural day, such as a town, temple or market visit.
  • Days 5 to 6: free days for water sports, spa time or day trips.
  • Day 7: pack, take a final swim and leave with time to spare.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking a destination without checking its December weather pattern.
  • Leaving flights and rooms to the last minute in peak weeks.
  • Packing too many activities and not enough rest.
  • Ignoring local rules, such as dress codes in temples and conservative areas.
  • Skipping insurance.

More Questions About Warm December Trips

Which warm destination is cheapest in December?

It depends on your departure point and dates. Thailand, Goa and Sri Lanka often offer good value compared with the Maldives, though all are pricier over Christmas and New Year.

Is December too hot for sightseeing?

In many tropical places, it is warm but comfortable, especially in the mornings and evenings. Plan outdoor activities for early hours and rest during midday.

Do I need vaccinations?

Requirements depend on your destination and nationality. Check official health advice well before you travel, and consult a doctor or travel clinic if you are unsure.

What should I pack for a beach trip?

Light clothing, swimwear, sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, insect repellent, a light jacket for evenings, a power bank and any personal medication.

Is it safe to swim in December?

Conditions vary. Follow local flag systems and lifeguard advice, avoid swimming alone and never swim after drinking alcohol.

How can I avoid crowded beaches?

Visit early in the morning, stay away from the busiest resort strips, and choose quieter bays. Weekdays are often calmer than weekends.

Should I choose a resort or a guesthouse?

Resorts offer convenience and facilities, and suit families and relaxed trips. Guesthouses are cheaper and more local, and suit travellers who want flexibility. Compare reviews and location.

How to Make a Final Choice

Narrow your options with three filters. First, weather: does the destination have its dry or summer season in December? Second, travel time: are you willing to spend a full day getting there? Third, budget: can you afford peak-week prices, or should you shift your dates?

Once you have two destinations left, compare real prices for your exact dates, read recent reviews and check the cancellation terms. Choose the one that fits your budget and has the most flexible booking, since plans can change.

Finally, keep your itinerary light. A beach holiday is about rest. Pick one or two activities, leave the rest of your time open and let the place set the pace.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is warm and sunny in December?

The Maldives, southwest Thailand, Goa, Sri Lanka's south and west coasts, and Cape Town are all typically warm and dry or sunny in December.

Is Bali good in December?

December is in Bali's wetter season, so expect more rain and humidity; it can still work with flexible plans.

Is it safe to travel to Fiji in December?

December is in Fiji's wetter, cyclone-prone season; many people still visit, but check forecasts and book flexible options.

What is the cheapest warm destination for Christmas?

Thailand, Sri Lanka and Goa generally offer better value than the Maldives, though all rise in price over the holiday weeks.

When are warm destinations most crowded?

From about December 20 to January 2, with peak pricing in those same weeks.

Should I book beach hotels early?

Yes, especially beachfront rooms in popular areas, which sell out well before Christmas.
