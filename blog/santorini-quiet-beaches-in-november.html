Santorini in July is a postcard, and also a queue. Santorini in November is something different: empty lanes, soft light, shuttered beach bars and a sea that has the whole shoreline to itself. If your idea of a perfect beach is one with no music, no sunbed hawkers and nobody in your photos, November has a lot to offer.

It also comes with trade-offs, and it is better to know them before you land. The water is cooler, daylight is short, many beach businesses have closed for the season and the weather can turn windy. This guide explains what to expect, which quiet beaches still make sense, how to get around on the winter bus programme and what to pack. If you are building a wider plan, our guide to Santorini coves you can reach without a car covers the routes in more detail, and our hidden coves of Santorini story shows the island at its best.

A note on accuracyWeather figures, opening hours and bus times come from travel guides and published schedules we read in November 2026. They change from year to year, and some details, such as which taverns are open, are only confirmed locally. Check before you travel, especially ferries, which can be cancelled by wind or strikes.

What November in Santorini Is Really Like

November sits between the end of the beach season and the start of winter, and the weather reflects that. One guide gives an average of about 17°C, with highs near 19°C and lows near 14°C. Another, using the nearby Naxos weather station as a guide, puts the mean daily maximum at about 18.8°C and the minimum at 13.7°C, with roughly 52 mm of rain falling over about nine rainy days. That is mild by northern standards, but it is not sunbathing weather for most people.

The bigger factor is wind. Autumn gales can cancel boat and ferry sailings, and a breeze that feels pleasant on a hotel terrace can make a beach feel much colder. Layers matter, and so does flexibility, because the best day for a beach walk may be the day you did not plan.

Daylight: Plan Around Early Sunsets

The short days catch many November visitors out. Using astronomical calculations, sunset falls around 5:21 pm on 1 November and by 30 November it is about 5:03 pm. Daylight shrinks from roughly 10 hours 40 minutes to just under 10 hours over the month, so a beach day has to start earlier than you might think.

DateSunriseSunset
1 November6:41 am5:21 pm
15 November6:55 am5:09 pm

Practically, that means doing your walking and swimming between late morning and mid-afternoon, saving sunset for a viewpoint and leaving your evening for dinner in Fira. State sites also close early, around 3:30 pm, so museum visits belong in the morning.

Can You Swim in Santorini in November?

Some people can, and some people will not want to. Travel writers put the sea at about 19 to 21°C in November, which is cool but not freezing, and one guide says swimming is possible for the committed. Another writer who visited found the water not particularly warm and too cold to swim comfortably. Both can be true, because your comfort depends on the wind, the sunshine and how cold-tolerant you are.

  • Go in on a calm, sunny day. Wind makes the air feel colder than the water, so the walk back to your towel is the hard part.
  • Keep it short. A quick dip is plenty, followed by a warm layer and a hot drink.
  • Swim near the shore and not alone. Do not count on lifeguards or beach services in the off-season, and stay where you can stand.
  • Try the hot springs instead. Boat tours to the volcanic islet are a warm alternative, with springs above 30°C all year according to one guide. Tours are reduced and weather-dependent in November, so check availability.

Which Quiet Beaches Make Sense in November

The beaches themselves are public and stay open all year. What changes is the beach infrastructure. Travel writers say Kamari, Perissa and Perivolos are largely shut by mid-month, meaning sunbeds, beach clubs and many seaside tavernas have closed. For many visitors that is exactly the appeal. Here is how each kind of beach feels in the off-season.

Kamari and Perissa promenades

Long, easy beaches reached by bus from Fira. Expect to walk on your own, with fewer cafes open. They are good for a winter stroll and a quiet coffee if something is open, and very easy to reach.

Monolithos

A calm, shallow, local-feeling beach that is quiet even in summer, so it feels properly deserted in November. Reach it by bus and a short walk, or by taxi.

Vlychada

Pale, sculpted cliffs and a lunar feel, which are even better without crowds. Facilities near the entrance may be closed, so bring what you need.

Katharos from Oia

A peaceful pebbly cove reached on foot from Oia, good for sunset and snorkelling in summer. In November, treat it as a short walk with a view rather than a swim.

Red Beach and White Beach need extra caution at any time of year because of landslide warnings, and boat access is reduced in the off-season. Our coves guide explains the safety notes in detail. If you are unsure, skip them and choose a calmer beach.

What Is Open and What Is Closed

November is the month when Santorini starts closing for winter, and it happens gradually. Many caldera hotels, beach-village restaurants and boat tours shut, and Oia thins out through the month. Fira is the best base, because it keeps the most services open all year. Late November to February is quieter still, with many restaurants and shops closed. Because opening depends on the owner, check each place before you rely on it, and book rooms with free cancellation.

Sightseeing hours are shorter too. These winter hours apply from 1 November to 31 March, according to one guide.

Most state sites are also closed on 25 and 26 December and 1 January. Wineries mostly work by reservation in winter, so book ahead if you want a tasting.

Getting Around: Winter Buses, Taxis and Ferries

Transport shrinks in winter, and that is the main practical challenge. A published winter programme for the island's bus company lists 26 departures a day between Fira and Oia, from 7 am to 9 pm, running every 30 minutes from 10 am. Fares from May 2026 were about 2.20 euros from Fira to Oia or to the airport, and 2.70 to the port at Athinios. The winter programme dates are not stated, so check when it starts and ends and what the last bus is for your route.

  • Buses are fewer. Beach routes may run less often than the Fira to Oia line, so confirm the schedule at Fira bus station.
  • Taxis are scarce late in the evening. Pre-book airport and port transfers, and arrange a return pickup if you go somewhere remote.
  • Ferries are weather-dependent. Winter timetables are thinner, and gales and strikes can cancel sailings. Leave a buffer day around your flight home.
  • A car is easier. Some guides suggest renting a car in winter because buses are fewer, but you can manage without one if you stay in Fira and plan around the timetable.

Early, Mid and Late November Compared

The month changes a lot between the first and last week. Open the tab that matches your dates.

Early November

More daylightMore places open

You still get the most light, with sunset around 5:20 pm, and more tavernas and boat trips are operating. Beach businesses have usually started closing, but you may find a few cafes still serving along the main strips. It is the best time for a mix of walking and sightseeing.

Mid November

Quiet, mixed weatherBeach clubs mostly shut

Travel writers say the main beach villages are largely shut by mid-month. You will find fewer people than at any other point of the season, and a wider choice of rooms. Plan flexibly for wind and rain, and keep your main outdoor plan for the calmest day.

Late November

Shortest daysMany shutters down

Sunset arrives around 5 pm, Oia is much quieter and many restaurants and shops are closing for winter. Base yourself in Fira, check each business before you travel and treat beach days as walks rather than swims.

A Sample November Beach Day

A quiet beach day in November works best when it is simple. Here is a relaxed pattern you can adapt.

  1. Check the weather and the bus. Look at the forecast the night before and note the last bus back.
  2. Start mid-morning. Take the bus to your beach once it has warmed up a little, and carry water, snacks and a warm layer.
  3. Walk first. Stroll the shoreline in the softest light and take your photos before the wind picks up.
  4. Swim only if it feels right. If the day is calm and sunny, take a short dip near the shore, then dress quickly.
  5. Have a long lunch in Fira. Choose a taverna you have confirmed is open, and enjoy the quiet.
  6. Finish with sunset. Pick a viewpoint and arrive before the sun drops, because it is gone by about 5 pm.

It is also sensible to check official updates from Greece's civil protection authority and your own government's travel advice before you go, as conditions can change.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Expecting beach clubs, sunbeds and busy tavernas in mid or late November.
  • Staying in Oia and finding most of it shut.
  • Leaving for a viewpoint late and missing sunset by half an hour.
  • Planning museum visits for the afternoon, when state sites have already closed.
  • Booking a tight ferry connection with no buffer day.
  • Swimming in strong wind or alone, with no lifeguard cover.
  • Assuming a taxi will always be available late in the evening.

Final Thoughts

Santorini's quiet beaches in November are for walkers, photographers and anyone happy to trade a hot swim for peace and soft light. Stay in Fira, plan around the early sunsets, keep your beach plans flexible and treat every open cafe as a bonus. Do that, and you will see the island the way few summer visitors ever do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is November a good time to visit Santorini?

It suits travellers who want quiet, soft light and low prices more than hot beach weather. Highs are around 19°C, daylight is short, many beach businesses have closed and wind can disrupt ferries, so keep your plans flexible.

Can you swim in Santorini in November?

Some people do. Travel writers put the sea at about 19 to 21°C, and one guide says swimming is possible for the committed, while another visitor found it too cold. Choose a calm, sunny day, stay near the shore and do not count on lifeguards.

Which beaches in Santorini are open in November?

The beaches are public and stay open, but beach clubs, sunbeds and many seaside restaurants close. Travel writers say Kamari, Perissa and Perivolos are largely shut by mid-month. Expect empty, quiet beaches with few facilities.

What time is sunset in Santorini in November?

About 5:21 pm on 1 November, 5:09 pm on 15 November and 5:03 pm on 30 November, according to astronomical calculations. Plan outdoor activities for late morning and the early afternoon.

Do buses run in Santorini in November?

Yes, on a winter programme. One published schedule lists 26 departures a day between Fira and Oia, but beach routes may be less frequent. Check the current timetable and the last bus at Fira station.

Is Akrotiri open in November?

Winter hours run from 1 November to 31 March, with Akrotiri open about 8:30 am to 3:30 pm. Sources disagree on its weekly closing day, so call or check the official site before you go.
