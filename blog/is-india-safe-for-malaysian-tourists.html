Is India Safe for Malaysian Tourists? What the High Commission Advisory Says

September 5, 20266 min readby WanderLust Blog

Malaysia and India maintain close, longstanding travel links, and a steady stream of Malaysian visitors make this trip every year, many connecting through Kuala Lumpur's frequent direct routes. Two things are genuinely specific to planning it from Malaysia: a visa mistake worth avoiding, and how the advisory system actually works here.

The Visa Mistake Malaysians Are Specifically Warned About

Malaysian passport holders need a visa to enter India — this isn't a visa-free arrangement, unlike some of Malaysia's regional travel relationships. The standard route is the e-Visa, applied for entirely online through India's official visa portal, with no embassy visit or physical document submission required. Malaysian citizens can apply for several e-Visa categories depending on purpose, including 30-day, one-year, and five-year tourist options, alongside separate business, medical, and conference categories.

Indian e-Visas are generally non-extendable and non-convertible, so selecting the correct visa category and purpose before applying matters — the Consulate-General of Malaysia in Chennai has specifically advised Malaysian travellers to ensure their visa matches their actual purpose of travel, following reported incidents of foreign nationals being denied entry over visa-purpose mismatches. Approval arrives by email as an Electronic Travel Authorization, which should be printed and carried for arrival in India.

How the Advisory System Actually Works

Malaysia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Wisma Putra) doesn't publish a fixed, numbered risk-level system for India the way some other countries do; instead, the High Commission of Malaysia in New Delhi maintains its own dedicated travel advisory page for Malaysians visiting India, and Wisma Putra has, at various points, issued specific guidance — including advising Malaysians to avoid areas affected by public protests when they occur, and general reminders to stay vigilant and prioritise personal safety.

  • Registering your trip through e-Konsular, Malaysia's official travel registration platform, is recommended before any overseas trip, including India, so the Ministry can reach you with updates or assistance if needed.
  • Consistent with the pattern seen in other countries' guidance for India, Jammu and Kashmir and areas near the India–Pakistan border warrant additional caution and are generally well outside standard first-timer circuits like the Golden Triangle, Rajasthan, or Kerala.
  • Since Malaysia's guidance for India is issued as specific advisories rather than a single ongoing risk rating, check the High Commission of Malaysia in New Delhi's travel advisory page directly and close to your travel dates, rather than relying on a static summary.

Getting There: Flights from Malaysia

India is served directly from Kuala Lumpur with frequent connections on multiple carriers to Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and other major cities, making Malaysia one of the more conveniently connected source markets for Indian travel. Given the relatively short flight time compared to long-haul markets, jet lag is less of a concern, though it's still worth allowing a settling-in day if your itinerary is packed — our India itinerary guide for first-time visitors covers how to structure a first trip either way.

Power Adapters: The Easy Part

Malaysia uses Type G plugs at 230V, while India uses Type C, D, and M sockets, also at 230V — so you'll need a plug adapter, but voltage conversion isn't a concern, since both countries run on the same voltage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is India safe for Malaysian tourists?

Malaysia's Wisma Putra doesn't publish a single fixed risk rating for India but maintains a dedicated travel advisory through the High Commission in New Delhi, generally recommending standard vigilance and registration via e-Konsular; major tourist circuits sit well outside the specific higher-risk border areas.

What visa mistake should Malaysians specifically avoid?

Applying for a visa category that doesn't match your actual purpose of travel — the Malaysian Consulate-General in Chennai has specifically flagged this after incidents of travellers being denied entry over visa-purpose mismatches.

Should I register with e-Konsular before traveling to India?

It's recommended — registering helps Malaysia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs reach you with relevant updates or assistance during your trip.

Will my Malaysian power adapter work in India?

Not directly — Malaysia uses Type G plugs and India uses Type C, D, and M, so you'll need a plug adapter, though both countries share the same 230V standard, so voltage conversion isn't required.

Is Jaipur or Delhi a good starting point for a first trip from Malaysia?

Delhi is the more common gateway given direct flight connections from Kuala Lumpur, and it connects easily onward to Agra and Jaipur as part of the classic first-timer Golden Triangle route.
