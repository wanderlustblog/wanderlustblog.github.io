Rovaniemi at Christmas: Santa Claus Village, Northern Lights and Practical Planning

October 21, 20269 min readby WanderLust Blog

Rovaniemi is the rare Christmas destination that actually looks like the postcard: deep snow, early dark and a village on the Arctic Circle that treats December as its main event.

Rovaniemi is built around winter. Tour operators run on dark-season timetables, hotels sell out for Christmas months ahead, and the town's biggest attractions are designed for cold, snowy days. Plan around that rhythm rather than against it.

Santa Claus Village

Santa Claus Village sits on the Arctic Circle and is the headline attraction, with Santa's office, a post office for sending Christmas cards stamped from the Arctic Circle, shops and cafés. According to the operator's published 2026 schedule, the Christmas House runs from November 23 to December 23 with extended hours on December 24 and 25, and Snowman World reopens on December 6 through March 2027. Dates and hours can change, so confirm on the official site before you travel.

Northern Lights: Realistic Expectations

The aurora can't be guaranteed. You need clear skies, darkness and some solar activity, and cloud cover is the usual spoiler. Give yourself several nights rather than one, choose an evening tour that can move to clearer skies if needed, and treat any sighting as a bonus on top of an already good trip.

Things to Do

  • Husky and reindeer experiences are the most popular family activities.
  • Snowmobile and snowshoe tours take you into the forest beyond the town.
  • Evening aurora tours run on clear nights through the season.
  • Local sauna and a warm meal make a good counterpoint to a cold day outside.

How to Get There

Rovaniemi has its own airport with connections via Helsinki, and an overnight train runs from Helsinki, which turns the journey into part of the trip. Santa Claus Village is a short bus or taxi ride from the town centre.

What to Wear

Layers matter more than any single heavy coat. Pack thermal base layers, a warm mid-layer, a windproof outer shell, insulated boots, warm socks, gloves and a hat. Many tour operators supply thermal overalls and boots, so check what's included before buying gear.

Booking Tips

Christmas in Lapland is a peak-demand product. Book flights, accommodation and your must-do activities first, and leave flexible space for aurora nights and rest days. For more ways to compare Christmas destinations, see our best places to visit for Christmas 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rovaniemi worth visiting for Christmas?

Yes, especially for families and anyone who wants a reliably snowy, festive setting with plenty of organised activities.

Can I be sure of seeing the northern lights?

No. You need clear skies and activity, so allow several nights and book tours that can chase clearer weather.

When should I book a Rovaniemi Christmas trip?

As early as possible; Christmas week is peak demand and hotels and tours sell out well ahead.

How do I get to Santa Claus Village?

It is a short bus or taxi ride from Rovaniemi's town centre, and also close to the airport.

What should I pack for Lapland in December?

Thermal layers, a windproof outer shell, insulated boots, gloves and a warm hat; some tours provide overalls and boots.

Are the attractions open on December 24 and 25?

Some are, with adjusted hours, but schedules vary, so confirm with each operator before you go.
